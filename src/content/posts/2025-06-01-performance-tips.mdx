## Speed matters

Some low-effort wins:

1. Code-split routes and heavy charts.
2. Lazy-load images and avoid layout shifts.
3. Memoize expensive calculations and components.
4. Prefer CSS animations over JS where possible.

Benchmark, measure, iterate.
